Gulmarg to Leh-Ladakh via Sonmarg and Kargil

By Bhupender Gupta · 21 December 2017

Generally, if you travel by Chopper, you get to see the spread of a lavish scenic platter in the hills as attractive as those in Gulmarg. However Gondola ride here is no less. From atop too, you get a wide perspective of the green carpet of pine trees and snow clad mountains below. They create a perfect Honeymoon with the incredible Nature approx. 4000 Mts above sea level. The last thing you experience before embarking on the journey from Gulmarg to Leh-Ladakh via Sonmarg and Kargil.

An unsolicited advice : Do not miss the Gondola when in Gulmarg.

Gulmarg (‘Gul’ meaning ‘Flower’), this beautiful Hill Station is christened for the variety of exotic flowers that bloom on its marvelous planes during flowering season. The other reason what this place is famous for is its steep ‘Skiing’ slopes. An ideal location for Skiing enthusiasts during winters.

Gondola at Gulmarg

Gulmarg’s Gondola has stations at two levels. To reach the first level, Kangdoori, situated at the altitude of 3099.60 Mts (above Sea level) and 2.5 Kms from the base station, the cable car cuts through the jungle of Pine trees, pierces through the descending clouds and takes approx. 10 Minutes to reach here.

Apharwat Peak is the station at the second level. This is situated at an altitude of 3979.50 Mts. Skiing from Phase 2 to Phase 1 is presumably the steepest and reputedly one of the most challenging slopes in the world. Unfortunately on the particular day, rains play spoil sport, thereby rendering the Cable Car from Phase 1 to Phase2 non-operational.

Nevertheless, as you alight at the Kangdoori station, the view outside is outstanding. The captivating surreal beauty of the Mountains from this vantage point can arguably sweep any nature lover off their feet. The fare up to Kangdoori is Rs 700/- per person & an extra Rs 900/- if you desire to go further to Apharwath.

The other places one can visit when in Gulmarg include ‘Indian Institute of Skiing & Mountaineering, ‘Maharani Shivji Temple’; and ‘Trout Fishing’ area. At ‘Trout Fishing Area’, you see hundreds of fat, healthy trout fishes swimming in a mid-size pond. You can fish any Three (Maximum allowed) and later hand them over to your hotel Chef for a delicious fish meal. Entry fees here is Rs. 500/- per person. Time limit : 3 hours.

Gulmarg to Leh-Ladakh via Sonmarg and Kargil

Early next morning, you embark upon the journey to Leh via Kargil in a pre-booked Car. Night halt at Kargil is advisable as it helps your body get acclimatize to the low levels of oxygen that increasingly deplete as you move towards Ladakh.

But before you reach Kargil, on the way is another amazing place you cannot and should not miss. This is the amazing, “Sonmarg” (Meadow of Gold), the last stop before you leave the Kashmir district.

Sonmarg

After a three hour drive from Gulmarg, you reach this stunningly beautiful hill station – Sonmarg. Park your car in one of the Parking lots alongside slew of restaurants & lodges and either trek or hire a pony to reach the most visited, the mesmerizing “Thajiwas Galciers”. A pony is hired for Rs. 2000/- to & fro.

In the next enthralling one hour thirty minutes, you see nature unfold as this pony slowly gallops over the soft green meadow grass on the curvy hilly slopes. Then pass through the Kuccha-Pucca trails alongside picturesque snow capped mountains till this amazing Glacier suddenly props up during the last turn.

Thankfully the amiable pony owner co-operates and helps to capture the captivating landscapes along the way.

At the glacier, tourists are seen making their way up this incredible silver snowy slope. So you too followed suit. Climbing this staggering Glacier up over 150 meters is an utterly enthralling experience. You find yourself surrounded by snow from all sides.

Now you have to climb down. It’s not that easy walking on the slippery snow. So you hire a sledge and speedy slide down the slope. This is really exciting hence the “Wow” was palpable.

Incidentally, Sonmarg is the shorter route from where Amarnath Yatris can trek to visit the highly revered Shiva Lingam at the Holy Shrine.

Back to the car, Amir, a young and seemingly experienced but aggressive speedster, zooms the car now towards Kargil. This stretch passes through some indescribable treacherous mountain terrains and Amir dangerously negotiates over these narrow strips of road almost nonchalantly.

Particularly scary was the 25 Kms slim stretch with surface of just lose stone and slushy mud below and intimidating steep valley on the right.

Kargil War Memorial

Nestled among arid mountains, at 5.30 pm, you reach the iconic Kargil War Memorial. This memorial was established to commemorate the victory of “Operation Vijay” during the Indo-Pak war of 1999.

So as you enter this extraordinary ‘Place of Pride’, you walk on the tarred corridor with Indian Flags fluttering on either side & Amar Jawan Jyoti right in the front. You almost instantaneously sense poignancy within you, remembering the umpteen martyrs who must have laid their lives on the line of duty to ensure that “WE”, ‘ We The People of India’, stay safe.

Don’t miss the Mangal Pandey Gallery inside to see gallants of our war heroes displayed and pay them their most deserving tribute. As you move to leave, deeper feel of pathos is engendered yet again reading the message at the exit that said:

“When you go home, tell them of us; and say, for your tomorrow, we gave our today” – How True.

Amir then drives you to Kargil for a night halt.

Kargil to Leh

Next morning, Amir picks you from the Hotel and drives towards the final destination, the city of Leh in Ladakh.

While on the way, the snow-capped mountains suddenly give way to arid but stunningly colorful Mountains. Each Mountain’s astonishing distinctive hue is just indescribable. You are compelled to be captivated by this nature’s exceptional marvel. Competing surreal scenes that cannot hold you back from your Camera Button. Click ! Click ! Click ! you continue till you reach the outskirts of Leh.

In between you halt briefly at the Fotula Top. Then after spending some quality time at the Famous “Lamayuru Gonpa”, the largest and oldest Tibetan Buddhist Monastery you pass by the intriguing Magnetic hills which are cyclops hills that create optical illusion of a hill wherein cars and objects on hill road appear to roll up in defiance of gravity when in fact they are rolling downhill.

Finally, on entering Leh, you see boards pointing to directions of Ladakh’s famous Monasteries. Later, passing by the historic Gurudwara of the Sikhs – “Patthar Saheb”, the – “Hall Of Fame” museum, Leh Airport and then crossing the local market you reach your impressive Hotel ‘The Grand Dragon – Ladakh’.

Over all, you are drained & tired as the journey from Gulmarg to Leh-Ladakh via Sonmarg and Kargil comes to an end. So after sumptuous dinner at the hotel’s restaurant you turn in to hit the bed for a much needed good-night sleep…. There I go….. Zzz……

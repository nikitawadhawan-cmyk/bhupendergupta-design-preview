LAS VEGAS – Where even Gods can gladly Gamble & Priests can Peacefully Play

By Bhupender Gupta · 9 February 2018

A Sin City ? A Gamblers Paradise ?? Indeed !! Las Vegas stands up to its fabled reputation every bit. Boozing, Gambling, Casinos, Night Clubs, Shows: decent, erotic, nude seem intrinsic & ubiquitous. As the Sun Sets, walking down this approx. 4 mile boulevard, more famously known as “The Strip” inadvertently and instantly, makes you a part of this exceptionally luminous night life. Every inch of this place is lit up to its T.

You feel Happy, Elated. The adrenaline in you pushes up. You feel rejuvenated !! Like they say “Do as the Romans do when in Rome.” It’s fun to metamorphose/camouflage yourself into this rare dazzle, this intriguing glamour, even if it is ephemeral, fleeting or temporary.

The Strip

Interestingly, most famous and palatial Hotels on this Strip, are built based on various themes. They include Venetian, Bellagio, Trump Tower, MGM, and others. They queue up one after the other, each one clamoring for attention and admiration. Restaurants, Fountains, men/women selling “Show” brochures, replicas of Eiffel Tower, Venetian Grand Canal, Egyptian Pyramid, fill the “Strip”.

You see happy faces, exuberance, animal spirit filled all around. At Bellagio, an exceptionally synchronized Fountain Show is something that’s impossible to miss. It’s free for every passerby to watch.

Just like you, you find everyone else around too taking pictures of this beautiful water concert.

Importantly, you can move freely into any of these huge hospitality structures. With ubiquitous Casinos in each, you see myriad gambling aficionados, young to old; males, females; White, Black, glued to their slot machines or on gambling tables, happily, either splurging or adding dollars to their kitty. It is a fete to watch the scenario.

Replete of Shopping arcades, you can seamlessly pass through from the premises of one hotel to another to another without realizing until you see the décor of the roof change. You will notice, each hotel with beautifully designed however distinct and discernible roof above.

Being a non-gambler, you rarely could comprehend how one could play on these amusing machines. It is intriguing. Watching you watching these machines with intrigue, an elderly lady comes to your succor. Unfortunately, she has just lost all her money into one slot machine. Nevertheless she explains the working of that machine till you understand; albeit somewhat.

You then flung yourself into it and sit on this machine to try your luck. But sadly, the result was the same as the new teacher. You too loose the entire amount that you had earmarked for Gambling. Nonetheless, you get up happy, proudly saying to yourself, “Yes, even you can, and have gambled in Las Vegas”.

As the real life on the scintillating strip picks up after stars begin to twinkle, there are some amazing things one can accomplish while the Sun is up & still shining.

Grand Canyon

Las Vegas can boast of the Grand Canyon being located in the same State in which Las Vegas is – that is Nevada, So you can reach the Canyon either by road, or on a small plane that will hover over the place and bring you back or else by an helicopter, that will not only fly over but also land 4,000 ft below the Canyon Rim, on its floor overlooking Colorado River.

Helicopter Tour to Great Canyon

So in order to have a complete feeling of the Canyon, you decide to travel on a 4 hour Helicopter tour. For this you pay $350 as fare and an extra $50 to sit beside the Pilot.

So as the rotor begins, this six-seated chopper takes off. There are several other Helicopters too going the same way. Soon you are flying over the famous Hoover Dam and then to over Lake Mead till you reached the mesmerizing Grand Canyon Rim.

After flying around for some time, the young, svelte lady pilot pulls the gear for a sharp descend into the canyon. In no time, you are flying just few feet above the flowing Colorado river. Subsequently, you land deep on the canyon floor, close to the river, The rays of the setting Sun has given an exceptional golden hue to the rocks. It seems as if divinity has lent a simmering sheet of Gold to this landscape. The look is indeed awe inspiring. Everyone’s Cameras gets at work and so is yours.

This is followed by Snacks and Champagne which is the part of the tour. Later, after 45 minutes, the helicopter takes off again to return back but not before allowing some astounding aerial view of the imposing Downtown Vegas and brilliantly illuminated Las Vegas Strip.

Hoover Dam

Hoover Dam, build at the man made Mead lake with waters from Colorado river, is situated around 35 miles from the Las Vegas strip. It is one of the 7 wonder of Industrial World and a place not to be missed when in Vegas.

Named after Mr. Hoover, being instrumental in getting this vital project through, this historic 726 feet high 1,244 feet long Dam with the top thickness of 45 feet and base of 660 feet, weighs astounding 6.6 million tons. (Equivalent to 18 Empire State Buildings, 685 Eiffel Towers and 29000 Statues of Liberty).

After self-drive to this place, you pay $15 and take a guided tour of this massive man-made marvel; a huge conical block of white cement that looks even more amazing as the shadow of an equally significant Memorial bridge falls across it.

Built by the consortium of Six big Companies with a budget of a whopping $48,890,955 in 1928, this dam got completed in May 1935 and was dedicated by the then President Franklin D. Roosevelt on 30th September 1935.

With this Dam getting operational, apart from generating 4 billion kilowatt-hour hydroelectric power a year, and providing potable water to countless Americans, it has also helped in controlling frequent floods and facilitated irrigation of over one million acres of land. It is presumed that without this Dam, the city of Los Angeles would not have been what it’s today.

Back at the strip

Once back at the strip, you realized the Sin City is not just filled with presumed conventional Sins. While staying at ‘The Venetian’, you attend a beautiful Show at ‘The Bellagio’, named “Cirque du Soleil O”.

With ticket at $15.95, this popular water show has exceptionally elaborated set that must have presumably cost millions of dollars. An immensely riveting experience ensues and everything seems so surreal.

Superbly talented acrobats and versatile artists display their mind blowing talents, often dangerous, keeping the audiences spellbound and in complete awe. Truly, a spectacle to behold. Unsurprising, the effect of the show continues to linger long after the curtains are drawn.

So, before bidding a final good bye to the glittering & glamorous Vegas, you take little time to visit the Madame Tussauds museum located just next to the Hotel.

With entry fees of $25, you find yourself standing along with famous Hollywood celebrities like Marilyn Monroe, Leonardo DiCaprio, Halle Berry,Julia Roberts, Britney Spears, Will Smith, Sport legends like Tiger Woods, Andre Agassi, TV Celebrities like Oprah Winfrey among many others who are brought to life through their incredibly striking Wax models.

The Car is ready at the Hotel’s porch and so are you. Press the ignition button and there you are; on a 5 hour drive towards the City of Los Angeles, along with the fond memories of this dazzling city – ‘Las Vegas’.

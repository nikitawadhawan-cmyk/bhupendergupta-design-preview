Cave your way to heaven @ Waitomo – NZ

By Bhupender Gupta · 21 August 2017

While tracking New Zealand from the northern to the southern islands by road, one is compelled to conclude that NZ is more than the land of Kiwis & Cricketers. It speaks of its grandeur through its surreal landscapes, enchanting lakes, picturesque mountains, pristine forests & most importantly, happy people. First leg : From Auckland to Waitomo Caves

New Zealand is a place to revel for any tourist wanting a perfect holiday. Three hour drive from Auckland are situated the magnificent Waitomo Caves (‘Wai’ in Maori means ‘Water & ‘tomo’ means ‘Shaft’) also known as Glowworm Caves.

This subterranean wonderland is renowned to be the most inspiring natural marvel of New Zealand. The uncanny lime stone formations in the grotto here, presumably begun some 30 Million Years ago. They suck water from the bottom of the ocean and grow every minute, even as you read this. It’s a fete to watch this ethereal miracle occur in front of your eyes.

For more fun and a bit of exploration you can coolly check-in in one of the motels build around the caves. With no shortage of space, you park your car, pay 49 NZ$ for an entry ticket (in case you don’t possess a discount coupon) and are soon escorted by a trained guide into these amazing caves for the next 45 minutes.

The mesmeric splendor begins to unfold as soon as you are ensconced below the caverns watching magical ‘stalactites & stalagmites’ (Both limestone deposits in the form of shafts. One hangs/grows from the roof & other rises upwards from the cave’s floor).

These ‘stalactites & stalagmites’ are supposedly endeavoring assiduously for thousands of years to assimilate into one-another. This indescribable optics and countless other dramatic lime stone manifestations add to the splendor as you walk softly through these fine caves towards the exit.

Along the way you see amazing Tiny sparks of light emitting from thousands of glow worms, also called bioluminescent insects. These are glued to the caves’ limestone walls and throw phosphorescent light from its 30-40 cms long glowing threads. A view good enough to take you into a trance and from there into a dreamland.

The boat ride at the end of this tour over the narrow lake that is rising from within the long eerie caves, makes your heart beat with excitement and awe. As this tiny boat is pulled forward holding the rope above, you get a sense of being transported to some fairy land.

You feel as if you are night-flying, up above the clouds in a star filled sky. The entire journey gets blissful & ecstatic and the only thing that comes out from your mouth is the whisper, surely heaven wouldn’t look better than this during any night.

Black-Water rafting, also called cave tubing, is open for enthusiasts who are extra adventurous, an experience that can make a visit even more memorable. The duration of Black Water Labyrinth is two hours and Black Water Abyss five hours.

Once you leave this amazing site, you are now towards your next halt ….. the thermal city of Rotorua, a three hour drive from Waitomo Caves, where spouting geysers, active volcanoes and sparkling lakes await you. And off you Go…………
